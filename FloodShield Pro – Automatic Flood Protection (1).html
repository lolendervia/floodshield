Step by step

From detection to sealed — in under two minutes

The system monitors water levels around the clock and responds automatically — no buttons, no manual action required.

01
💧
Water detected

The integrated sensor detects rising water and sends an instant signal — including during power outages via battery backup.

02
⚙️
Pump activates

The side-mounted pump builds air pressure. Its weather-sealed housing is compact and virtually maintenance-free.

03
⬆️
Barrier rises

The inflatable barrier travels up corrosion-resistant guide rails from bottom to top, covering the entire window opening.

04
🔒
Window fully sealed

Fully inflated, the barrier forms a watertight wall. Once the flood passes, it deflates and resets automatically.

Technical features

Engineered for long-term reliability

Every component is selected for durability under continuous wet and corrosive conditions.

🛡
Corrosion-resistant guide rails

Treated aluminium profiles — permanently tight even in salt water and debris-heavy runoff.

🌬
Pneumatic lifting mechanism

Air pressure drives the barrier — no electric motor in the wet zone. More robust and lower maintenance than hydraulic systems.

📡
Wi-Fi water level sensor

Connects to your smart home, sends push alerts, and can trigger third-party alarms before water reaches your window.

🔧
Retrofit — no structural changes

Mounts to the exterior wall without modifying the window frame or masonry seal.
